## Full description (Amazon / KDP — paste as plain text or simple HTML)
**Can Aarav save enough for his dream bicycle?**

Eight-year-old Aarav sees a shiny red bicycle in a shop window, and it costs ₹5,000! With a pink piggy bank from his little sister Anya, three money jars from Dad and a visit to the bank, Aarav sets out on a journey that teaches him far more than how to save.

Along the way he spends his savings on a video game (oops!), learns the Pause Rule, builds good habits, leads a science-fair team, faces failure, helps a friend in need and even starts a little bookmark business. Will he reach his goal?

**Inside this book, children discover:**
- The difference between needs and wants
- Saving, spending wisely and sharing with three money jars
- What a bank does and how interest works (explained with a clearly labelled pretend example)
- Setting smart goals and managing time
- Good habits, reading and patience
- Leadership, teamwork and kind communication
- How to learn from mistakes and failure
- Kindness, honesty and helping others

**Perfect for reading together:** every page has a short memorable quote and a *Think & Talk* question. The book ends with 10 Golden Life Lessons, fun activities, a parent–child discussion guide and **6 printable worksheets**: My Money Goal, Needs or Wants?, My Three Money Jars, a 7-Day Good Habit Tracker, a Daily Routine Planner and My First Little Business.

Money is a helpful tool, but this story reminds children that family, health, kindness and learning are the greatest treasures of all.

*Amounts are shown in Indian rupees (₹). This book shares general ideas about money for children and is not financial advice.*
